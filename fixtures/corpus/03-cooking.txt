To cook rice well, rinse the grains until the water runs clear, then soak them for twenty minutes before cooking. Heat oil in a heavy pot, fry garlic and onion until golden, add the rice and stir to coat every grain, then pour in water and salt and bring it to a boil. Cover the pot, lower the heat, and let it steam undisturbed until the water is absorbed and the grains are tender and separate.
